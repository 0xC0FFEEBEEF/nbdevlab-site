A note in the quiet.

The opening piano recording is from Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, distributed by the Tone.js audio collection under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

The recording is transposed in the score, softened, and placed in a long stereo reverb. Atmospheric sound and the radio processing were composed with Web Audio and rendered into the seekable soundtrack. No endorsement is implied.

Radio chatter is cut into short, rearranged fragments and filtered from NASA’s Historical Sounds collection: “Good Picture of Steve,” “Houston Discovery,” and “How Do You Read.” It is used as fictional background comms, with no NASA endorsement implied.

Earth imagery: NASA, distributed in the Three.js examples texture collection. The Moon surface texture from the same collection provides asteroid surface detail.
